About me

I’m Anthony “Tony” Aiello. I like taking a problem from the first conversation through requirements, code, testing, and deployment.

How I got here

My first technical work was in audio engineering and live production. That led to managing technical operations, building an online storefront for a Milwaukee sound and lighting business, and working in enterprise IT. Across those roles, I learned to ask who depends on a system and what they need it to do.

In software, I’ve written C for a microcontroller, connected an online bookstore to its backend, and worked with a team to build a planning tool using MongoDB, Express, Vue, and Node.js. I use Git, Jira, user stories, and Kanban boards to organize work and adapt when requirements change.

I’m now looking for a role in software development, cybersecurity, or systems where I can bring those skills together.
